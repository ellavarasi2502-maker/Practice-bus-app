# Practice-bus-app
Practice Making Bus App — a one-page web app for getting to school on bus 99.

Open `index.html` on your phone (or host it with GitHub Pages) and "Add to Home Screen".

- **Morning** — tells you *when to leave home* for the next bus 99, using live arrival times,
  your 10-min walk, and the 6:30am deadline. Also shows a "safe" leave time that works even
  if you just missed a bus (13-min gap).
- **After school** — every bus at the school stop with countdowns and "walk out in X min".
- **Settings** — save your home & school stop codes (or tap "Find stops near me"), bus number,
  walk/ride times. Saved on your phone only.

Try it with fake data: open `index.html?demo`.

Live times come from the free arrivelah API (LTA data), stop names from busrouter.sg.
